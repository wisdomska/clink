# Changelog

All notable changes to Clink. Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-13

First release.

### Added

- Accounts: email and password, magic link, Google, passkeys; date-of-birth age gate; invite codes and links to pair with exactly one person; unlink with a 30 day read-only window; data export and account deletion.
- Us: send a clink, presence, dual clocks, moods, countdowns on a split-flap board, daily rituals, hold together.
- Chat: text, photos, video, voice notes with waveforms, stickers, reactions, replies, offline queue.
- Play: How Well Do You Know Me, Draw and Guess, Trivia duel, Would You Rather, a turn-based word game and Story builder, all refereed on the server.
- Together and Plans: watch parties with synced YouTube, listen together, date kits, date roulette, 30 day challenges with streaks and badges.
- Keep: letters to open later, time capsules, a shared timeline.
- After Dark: PIN lock, mutual opt-in, tiered consent with both partners' agreement, dare wheel and deck, private vault with watermarked media, panic wipe.
- Safety: reports, blocks, quiet hours, discreet notifications.
- Admin: TOTP-protected dashboard with aggregate metrics and free-tier usage, report queue, user tools, content editor, audit log.
- PWA: installable, offline shell, Web Push.
- QA: unit, RLS, end-to-end (two partners, three viewports), accessibility and Lighthouse checks in CI.
